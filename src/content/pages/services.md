### Space planning

At DeClaire Design, we begin by listening and asking questions. Learning how your family and guests move through your space helps us plan layouts that work for the way you live. We map traffic flow, test multiple layouts, and deliver scaled floor plans before a single piece is ordered.

### Furniture, fixtures, equipment and color

We select furnishings, lighting, finishes, and color palettes that look beautiful and stand up to heavy daily use. Healthier, longer-lasting materials mean less maintenance and fewer replacements. We handle sourcing, ordering, and delivery coordination.

### 3D renderings

See your space before it's built. Our 3D models and renderings let you walk through layouts, finishes, and lighting, so you can make confident decisions and avoid costly changes later.

### Hospitality design

We also design select boutique hospitality spaces: tasting rooms, restaurants, and small hotels in wine country. Our concept designs, such as The Velvet Hour, show our approach to layout, lighting, and materials for places people want to linger.
