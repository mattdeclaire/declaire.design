**Katie DeClaire**, Principal Designer

Over 20 years in the Bay Area. Founder of DeClaire Design LLC.

Hi! I'm Katie, founder of DeClaire Design, a residential and boutique commercial interior design studio in Benicia. We design restaurants, hotels, and living spaces that feel very California: curated, effortless, and inviting. We listen first, then handle everything from concept and space planning to furnishings and installation. You get a space that works for your guests and staff, built with natural, longer-lasting finishes, a project that stays on schedule and on budget.

**Listening first. Designing thoughtfully.** We'd love to work with you on your next project.
