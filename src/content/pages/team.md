**Katie DeClaire**, Principal Designer

Over 20 years in the Bay Area. Founder of DeClaire Design LLC.

Hi! I'm Katie, founder of DeClaire Design, an interior design studio in Benicia. We design homes that feel very California: curated, effortless, and inviting. We listen first, then handle everything from concept and space planning to furnishings and installation. You get a space that works for how you live, built with natural, longer-lasting finishes, and a project that stays on schedule and on budget. We also design select boutique commercial spaces.

**Listening first. Designing thoughtfully.** We'd love to work with you on your next project.
