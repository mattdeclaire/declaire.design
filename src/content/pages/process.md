---
title: Our process
description: How a DeClaire Design project works, from the first conversation to installation.
heading: Our process
---

1. **Listen.** We start with a conversation about your home, your family, and how you want to live in the space.
2. **Plan.** We measure your space and develop floor plans that fit how you live and your budget.
3. **Design.** We choose the color palette, materials, furnishings, and lighting, then show you the design in 3D renderings.
4. **Install.** We order, track, and coordinate delivery of everything, then oversee installation so the finished space matches the plan.
