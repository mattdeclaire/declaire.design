# Brand

## Voice

- First person, from Katie ("I'm Katie", "I can help you"). Her 2023 copy is warm, direct and unpretentious.
- Plain words over design jargon. "A space you love to come home to" rather than "curated, elevated environments".
- Short sentences. No hype, no superlatives she wouldn't say out loud.
- **Never** put words in Katie's mouth. No invented quotes, credentials, years of experience or client testimonials.

### Photography

- Katie's project photos are the heart of the site. Let them be big.
- Most are landscape. The Mid-Century project mixes portrait and landscape, so layouts must handle both.
- Some photos were taken by a professional photographer (Tahoe Cabin, likely real-estate listing photos). Check usage rights before publishing them.
- Strip GPS and location data from every photo before committing it.
